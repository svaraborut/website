For years the question "where do you write?" has had the same answer here: nowhere, really. A few threads, a handful of notes that never escaped Notion, the occasional reply long enough to feel guilty about. None of it lived anywhere I could point to.

So this is the first post on a small, intentional space for longer thoughts. Not a content engine. Not a newsletter. Just a place where ideas that need more than three paragraphs can sit and be linkable.

## What you'll find here

The bar is simple: things I'd want to read myself a year from now. Mostly that means notes from building software at the edge of AI and product — what worked, what didn't, the patterns I keep reaching for. Some posts will be short. Some will probably be too long.

Expect occasional detours into:

- Engineering decisions that turned out to matter more than they looked
- How small teams actually ship without burning out
- The unglamorous infrastructure that makes the glamorous stuff possible
- Tools and primitives I keep building because the existing ones don't quite fit

## What you won't find

No SEO bait. No "10 things" posts. No AI-generated filler. If something here reads like it was written to be ranked rather than read, it's a bug — please tell me.

## Cadence

Whenever a thought is ready, not before. The web has enough scheduled content.

Thanks for being here early.
